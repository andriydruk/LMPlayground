LM Playground runs open language models entirely on the device. The app ships without a model, because models are hundreds of megabytes to several gigabytes; you download one from Hugging Face inside the app. There is no account or sign-in.

To try it:
1. Open the app and tap "Choose a model" at the top of the chat, then Browse Models (also in Settings > Models).
2. Tap Download next to Gemma 3 1B, the smallest model (806 MB). Wi-Fi is recommended.
3. When the download finishes, choose Gemma 3 1B in the model picker and send a message.

To try photos, download Gemma 3 4B (2.5 GB) with "With image understanding", choose it, and attach a photo with the + button.

Models are data files (GGUF weights), not executable code. They run in the llama.cpp engine built into the app.

Nothing is collected or sent anywhere: chats, photos, documents and dictated audio stay on the device. The network is used only to download model files from huggingface.co and, if you turn them on in Settings > Tools (off by default), for the optional web search (DuckDuckGo) and web page fetch tools.

The app needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M1 chip or later.
